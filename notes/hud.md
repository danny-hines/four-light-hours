## Round 2 (critic round 2 -> this round)

- **Three viewport forms** (`formFor(W,H)` in hud.js, mirrored by the media queries): `wide`; `narrow` (portrait
  phone, <= 760 px wide and not short); `short` = landscape and <= 520 px tall (a phone on its side, a letterbox
  window). In `short` the unit drops below 1 (`--u: clamp(0.62px, 100vh/560, 1px)`, same formula in `unitFor`),
  small caps keep px floors, the **telemetry is one dim line along the top** (key over value: AU, light-time, speed,
  clock, target + altitude; the light-time ruler becomes a 5 px hairline above it), cards drop the data line, the
  closing card is compacted, and the short card form is a near candidate rather than a last resort.
- **Controls never collide with text**: while the transport is shown, `--lift` (= row top + 10u - `--mb`, measured)
  raises the card, caption, slate and closing card (`margin-bottom`, eased .45 s); it is 0 at 16:9 desktop sizes.
  The controls band is also a hard obstacle (and fade zone) for labels.
- **Telemetry steps back over the subject**: opacity x (1 - 0.45 coverage) where a body disc / limb band lies under
  its box, unless the subject fills the whole frame (then legibility wins).
- **Numbers agree**: a caption altitude ("400 km up - ...") is checked against the director's real altitude over the
  caption; if it is not the camera's height it is rewritten to describe the orbit ("A low orbit 400 km up sees a
  sunrise every 90 minutes"), or corrected if the camera holds another steady height. The clock row is **SIM. CLOCK**;
  from the finale it eases into **EPOCH · 2 Nov 2026 12:00 · planet positions** (the positions the film uses). TARGET
  reads altitude near a world and **distance** beyond 100 radii / 0.01 AU; for the Sun at a distance the sub-line is
  dropped (FROM THE SUN already says it).
- **Labels**: the director's crowding / limb dimming is not shown as ghosts. Moon labels take their beat's own fade;
  other dimmed labels are either shown or not (short ramp). A name must be nearer its own point than any other disc
  (or, without a leader, any other point), never in the Sun's glare (a disc of rpx x 1.4 + 7.5 % of the 16:9 height when the Sun is a
  small unoccluded point, so "THE SUN" goes outside on a leader), never under the controls. Points within 30u of the
  frame edge fade and points outside it are culled (no stray name with a leader running off screen). A reticle whose
  ticks would touch visible text fades with it.
- **Legibility shadows** instead of the corner vignette: one solid, heavily blurred box per line of a card/caption
  (blur proportional to the type size, so adjacent lines melt into one soft shadow hugging the text), opacity
  <= 0.34 x the light meter's reading, colour = the meter's mean colour behind the block, darkened and slightly
  saturated (behind a photosphere caption the shadow is brown-orange, on Venus warm). Text halos are layered
  (1 px, 3u, 4-10u, 16-30u) and scaled by `--halo`.
- **Early exit**: per beat and viewport, if the subject is clear of the chosen text as the fade begins but reaches it
  before the fade ends, the block leaves 0.45 s ahead of it (`layout.exit`).
- **Finale orbit lines** are soft obstacles in the composer (projected Keplerian ellipses rasterised to an 8u grid
  while `shot.orbits` > 0).
- **Cards**: three facts (`FACT_KEYS`; no "mean distance" next to the telemetry's true one), equal fixed-width
  columns (`--nf`), balanced wrapping, proper names kept together ("Olympus Mons"), shorter wording for five long
  values (`FACT_SHORT`; hud.js uses a short value only if its numbers are exactly the fact's), the title at 4.1vw
  (about 5 % cap height), and two beats: the name and its line first, facts from +2 s while the line steps back to
  74 %. `f-narrow` with three facts spans the third across the row.
- **Chapter slates**: "II — MERCURY" in the card's corner from 0.5 s after a warp-led chapter starts until its card
  begins, so every chapter is named from its first second (`__hud.beats.slates`).
- **Timeline**: a chapter name that does not fit its segment uses `TIMELINE_SHORT` (Opening, Sun, Earth, Belt,
  Finale); the current one stays clamped inside the track.
- Live cost: median 0.7 ms, p95 1.2-1.4 ms (`playcost.mjs` at 40 s and in the finale). Cached text boxes now shift
  with the letterbox instead of being re-measured on every frame of a matte animation.

## What exists

- **Subject-aware placement (layout.js + chooseLayout in hud.js).** Every title card and caption is placed, once per
  beat and viewport size, where its *text* stays off the film's subject. For ~10 frames spread over the beat
  (`director.evaluate` is pure) the composer projects every body/moon sphere (exact ray-sphere, lit side weighted
  over night side, a 22·u px padding band so text clears limbs and prominences), Saturn's rings, the Sun's disc +
  glare (only if no body hides it) and the director's labelled points. Candidate placements are measured on an
  invisible clone of the block (line boxes of each row, not the bounding box) and scored by covered text area,
  weighted by the block's own fade envelope and by stage (the name shows from the arrival, facts at the beat).
  Candidates in preference order (bias = what a candidate must save): lower-left, title 15 % tighter,
  lower-right (mirrored: right-aligned, rule/kicker/tracking from the right), narrower reflow (2x2 facts),
  single column, upper-left, and the short name+subtitle form only when every placement is heavily covered.
  Phones: lower, below-telemetry upper, short form. Body view never uses the short form. Placements are
  precomputed in idle slices after the first frame (nearest upcoming beat first), so a card never costs a frame
  when it appears; capture computes them on first use (same result). `window.__hud.layout[key]` has the
  candidate table; `hudlayout=1` draws the subject field (red) and the chosen text rows (cyan) for debugging.
- **Splash**: FOUR LIGHT-HOURS, "Sunlight takes about four hours to reach the last planet. Follow it.", a 1 px
  progress hairline fed by `setLoading`, BEGIN (calls `app.start()`; Enter/Space work too), "Headphones recommended",
  "Positions computed for <epoch, UTC>". Right (top on phones): an **orrery of the real configuration on the epoch** —
  true Keplerian orbit shapes from `astro.js`, seen from ecliptic north, logarithmic radial scale (captioned as such),
  a main-belt scatter, and the dashed straight-line route Sun → Mercury → … → Neptune. On 2 Nov 2026 it shows Venus
  just inside Earth (inferior conjunction). Names are an HTML layer, de-overlapped and kept on screen. Capture mode:
  only with `splash=1`, rendered in its finished, loaded state.
- **Beats** (when each text block shows) come from the director's caption list:
  `kind:'title'` → cold open, `kind:'body'` → title card, `kind:'note'` (or untyped) → caption, `kind:'credits'` →
  closing card. Without typed captions they fall back to the chapters. `window.__hud.beats` lists them.
- **Cold open**: centred FOUR LIGHT-HOURS, letters rise in one by one, a hairline draws from the centre, the
  director's subtitle and date line follow. Telemetry fades in only as the intro chapter ends.
- **Title cards** (lower-left third): roman numeral of the chapter + classification ("III — Terrestrial planet"), the
  name in thin wide-tracked Jost (letters stagger in while the tracking settles), a hairline, a number-free poetic
  subtitle (`copy.js`), 3 facts **only from `BODIES[id].facts`**, and one line of the director's computed data that
  the facts don't already state (deduplicated by quantity+unit and wording), e.g. "0.33 AU from the Sun · sunlight
  takes 2 min 43 s" under "Mean distance 0.39 AU", or "Right now its north pole faces the Sun". **Arrival stage:**
  when a chapter opens with a warp leg, the name appears 0.8 s after the chapter start, as the destination swells
  from a point of light; subtitle, facts and data follow at the director's beat. Long names shrink to fit 44 % of the
  width so they don't cover the subject. All pure functions of `t`.
- **Captions** (notes): kicker = note title, text = its lines (Cormorant italic, per-word fade), a small data line from
  its subtitle. They cross-fade with the title card so the two never overlap.
- **Closing card** (credits beat, over the director's fade to black): FOUR LIGHT-HOURS, "The planets were drawn at
  their true sizes and distances, where they really were on <date>." plus a sentence **computed from the real
  light-times and the film's duration** ("Sunlight that left the Sun as this film began has passed Venus, and has not
  yet reached us."), a credits grid, and "Watch again" once the film has ended.
- **One scaling unit**: `--u` = clamp(1px, min(100vh/900, 100vw/1440), 2.6px) drives every font size, offset,
  icon and gap (1 at 1600x900, 2.4 on a 4K TV at 1:1, 0.62-1 in the short landscape form); hairlines use `--hl`,
  a whole pixel count set from JS.
  JS layout constants (labels, scrims, title fit) use the same factor (`unitFor`). Small caps labels >= 0.6 alpha.
- **Numbers**: the director's light-time clauses are re-parsed and rewritten in the telemetry's own format
  ("78 min 23 s" -> "1 h 18 min 23 s", exact from the real distance); a data-line clause whose every quantity the
  telemetry already shows beside it (camera distance / light-time at the beat, within 1 % or the clause's
  rounding) is dropped. "above photosphere" becomes "distance" beyond 0.05 AU. Running text gets no-break spaces
  in dates and between numbers and units, and `text-wrap: balance` on subtitles, captions and the cold open.
- **Telemetry** (top-right; 3-column grid on phones): distance from the Sun (AU + km), light-time from the Sun, a
  **linear light-time ruler** with ticks at every planet's real distance on the epoch (the inner planets crowd the
  first pixels — that's the point), apparent speed (`shot.speedC`; differentiated from `director.evaluate` only if a
  director reports none), simulated UTC date/time + "1 s of film = 20 min", target + altitude above its surface.
- **Labels** for `shot.labels`: hairline reticle sized to the projected disc + name (+ sub line). Culled when behind
  the camera, off screen, behind the letterbox matte, occluded by any body/moon sphere, or when the body fills the
  view. Crowded points (the inner planets from 46 AU) get shrunken reticles and names fanned out on hairline
  leaders. Hard obstacles (rejected, never penalised) for names *and* leaders: the HUD's text rows (card rows,
  caption rows, telemetry; inflated 6·u px), every body/moon disc wider than 4 px other than the label's own,
  other reticles/names/leaders (exact segment-rect, segment-circle, segment-segment tests), and a safe area
  (half the side margin, clear of the matte). A point that lies under visible text fades with it
  (alpha x (1 - text alpha)). Each name gets a halo from the analytic brightness behind it (glare, lit limbs).
  `L` toggles a planetarium overlay labelling every planet with its distance from the camera.
- **Arrival**: a chapter's name appears only once the destination's disc actually reads (>= 16 px across at 900 px
  tall, inside the frame, clear of the Sun's disc + glare); until then a reticle labelled "<Name> / Arriving" marks
  the point of light it is coming from (`beats.arrivals`). If the disc never reads before the beat, the name comes
  with the full card.
- **Legibility**: a **light meter** reads the finished frame (after bloom/flare) behind each text block — synchronous
  in capture stills (exact), asynchronous PBO + fence during playback (no GPU stall) — combined with an analytic
  estimate (ray–sphere tests against every body/moon with albedos and the real Sun direction, Saturn's ring plane,
  solar glare). It drives per-line blurred shadows (cards, captions; tinted from the meter's colour), corner scrims
  for the telemetry and the centred blocks, and an adaptive text halo (`--halo`). Invisible over space.
- **Letterbox**: landscape -> text moves inside the 2.39:1 picture (`--lb`); portrait phones -> text sits in the tall
  bars like subtitles in a matte; the cold open splits: FOUR LIGHT-HOURS + rule inside the picture strip (font
  fitted to the strip), its subtitle and date line in the lower bar.
- **Timeline + controls** (auto-hide after 3 s idle while playing; shown while paused): play/pause/replay,
  elapsed/total, current chapter, chapter ticks + names (hidden when their segment is too narrow; the current one
  always shows, clamped inside the track), scrub by click/drag (mouse, pen, touch; pauses while dragging), hover
  tooltip, labels / mute / HUD / fullscreen buttons, cursor hides when idle. Click on the picture = play/pause,
  double-click = fullscreen, tap = show/hide controls.
- **Keyboard** (capture-phase handler, so a focused button never swallows Space; mouse clicks blur buttons, Tab
  focus is kept): Space/K play-pause · ←/→ previous/next chapter (← restarts the current one if >2.5 s in) ·
  Shift+←/→ ±10 s · M mute · F fullscreen · H titles+telemetry · L planet labels · Home/End. Timeline focused:
  ←/→ = 5 s steps.
- **Fonts**: Jost (variable 200–600) + Cormorant Garamond Italic (variable 400–600), Google Fonts latin subsets
  (SIL OFL), embedded as base64 (~88 KB) and registered with FontFace before the first frame → offline-safe and
  deterministic in capture. Fallbacks: Futura/Avenir Next/Century Gothic/Segoe UI; EB Garamond/Garamond/Palatino/
  Georgia. `window.__hud.fonts` = `{ ok, families, error }`.
- **prefers-reduced-motion** (or `motion=0`): opacity fades only. `ctx.visible === false`: overlay hidden, splash kept.
- `view=body`: the same card the film shows for that body (or `target=` moon), full opacity, + telemetry.
- Cost: ~0.3 ms median / 1.6 ms p95 per frame during playback (`node src/ui/dev/playcost.mjs`). In capture stills
  `window.__hud.meterMs` is the synchronous readback waiting for the GPU frame (not HUD work).

## Formats the HUD reads (for the director)

- `chapters[i]`: `{ id, title, body?, t0, t1, subtitle?, kicker?, card? }` (`card:false` suppresses a fallback card).
- `director.captions` (full list) and `shot.captions` (active ones): `{ t0, t1, kind, id?, title, subtitle?, lines[] }`
  as above; also accepts `{ text, sub?, alpha? }` or strings.
- `shot.labels`: ids or `{ id?, name?, sub?, position? (universe Vector3 | [x,y,z]), radius?, alpha?, priority?, kind? }`.
- `shot.speedC`, `shot.simDays`, `shot.focus`, `shot.fade`, `shot.letterbox` (2.39:1 matte, as post draws it).

## Known gaps

- Finale card: the composer now counts the orbit lines, but no corner is free of them in the 46 AU pull-back, so
  Neptune's faint orbit still runs through the right end of "THE SOLAR SYSTEM" (lower-right is still the cheapest
  placement by 2.5x). A finale framing with the right third clear of Neptune's ellipse would fix it.
- Placement and exits are chosen per beat from the director's path at load (and on resize); they follow the director
  automatically. Placement is geometric: a lens-flare ghost or the Milky Way is not "subject" (the meter + shadows
  handle legibility there).
- Label placement is re-solved per frame (deterministic, no hysteresis), so a name can hop sides when points cross.
- In the short landscape form the telemetry line is content-sized; its box is re-measured whenever its text length
  changes (deterministic).
- The light meter only sees the canvas.
